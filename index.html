About Ayushree Ayurved Panchakarma Hospital

Empowering health through time-tested Ayurvedic wisdom, in the heart of Buldhana.

Our Vision

To make authentic Panchakarma and holistic Ayurvedic care accessible, effective and affordable, guiding every patient toward lasting balance and wellness.

Led by Dr. Prajyot Wagh and Dr. Bhagyashri Patil (Wagh), Ayushree Ayurved Panchakarma Hospital combines classical Panchakarma protocols with focused expertise in Women's Health & Lifestyle care.

  • Personalized dosha-based treatment plans
  • Dedicated Panchakarma therapy unit
  • Compassionate, patient-first approach

Panchakarma Therapies We Offer

A holistic range of Panchakarma treatments to cleanse and rejuvenate body, mind & spirit

Vamana

Therapeutic emesis to cleanse Kapha toxins from stomach & lungs.

Virechana

Purgation therapy that clears Pitta toxins and purifies the blood.

Basti

Medicated enema therapy - the cornerstone treatment for Vata disorders.

Nasya

Nasal administration of herbal oils for head, sinus & neck conditions.

Shirodhara

Warm herbal oil stream over the forehead for stress, insomnia & migraines.

Kati Basti

Warm oil retained over the lower back to relieve chronic back pain & sciatica.

Janu Basti

Localized knee oil therapy for arthritis, stiffness & joint pain.

Udwarthanam

Herbal powder deep-tissue massage for detoxification & weight management.
